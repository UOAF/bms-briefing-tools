# Event 744 Player Briefing

## Game Plan
**Situation.** Event 744 continues the fight around Daegu. Prior-event material is context only; current package tasking and the 744pre save govern this brief.

Protect friendly forces and break the enemy maneuver on the Daegu front while stripping the remaining strategic SAM cover and denying Pohang as a fighter source. Low missile supply is a general theme across the SA-10 target set: Alpha and Bravo are assessed RED, while North is assessed BLACK. Package 6939 exploits that depletion to suppress the mobile/coastal SA-10s—Hawkeye 6 can reliably attack SA-10 North from altitude—while Package 6957 maintains continuous killbox interdiction under a dedicated local TARCAP.

### PKG 6939 - SEAD
Strategic air-defense suppression and forward fighter screen. Low SA-10 missile supply is a general feature of the target set: Alpha and Bravo are assessed RED, while North is assessed BLACK. Plasma 3 destroys mobile SA-10 Alpha and Bravo; Hawkeye 6 destroys coastal SA-10 North; Devil 1, Eagle 5, and Lancer 7 protect the attacks and then press toward Pohang when the relevant SAM gate is open.

- Fallback: The Eagle screen defaults to the safe western patrol geometry at STPT 3-4. If Alpha or Bravo is active, the Eagles remain west until Plasma 3 confirms its DEAD tasking complete. STPT 5-6 are the desired forward patrol points but lie inside SA-10 North's threat area; the Eagles may use them only after SA-10 North is confirmed down and Alpha/Bravo are no longer factor. If SA-10 North never goes down, all three Eagle flights remain at STPT 3-4 and do not press Pohang.
- Planner contracts: Plasma 3: Find and destroy SA-10 Alpha and Bravo; Hawkeye 6: Destroy SA-10 North; Devil 1: Western safe patrol, then forward patrol and Pohang sweep; Eagle 5: First-arriving western safe patrol, then forward patrol and Pohang sweep; Lancer 7: Safe northeast block, then forward northeast patrol

### PKG 6957 - AI
Killbox interdiction package. The three relevant SA-10 battalions are generally missile-depleted—Alpha and Bravo are assessed RED, while North is assessed BLACK—but remain factor threats until confirmed neutralized. Spade 7 holds local TARCAP over the killboxes while four AI flights attack enemy maneuver forces on the western/forward edges of Killboxes 1-3. AI execution is independent of the Alpha/Bravo SEAD result; if those SA-10s are active, use low-altitude pop-up attacks with standoff PGMs rather than waiting.

- Fallback: All AI flights attack regardless of the Alpha/Bravo status. If Alpha or Bravo is active and factor, execute low-altitude pop-up attacks with standoff PGMs; do not hold or wait for the SA-10s to be destroyed.
- Planner contracts: Mudhen 7: Interdict west Killbox 1 lead elements; Plasma 7: Interdict southwest Killbox 3 lead elements; Cyborg 5: Reattack Mudhen 7 target area; Hammer 6: Interdict west Killbox 2 lead elements; Spade 7: Killbox TARCAP

### Execution Gates
- Plasma 3 works south of Killbox 3, then north onto SA-10 Alpha and Bravo; its masked route must remain clear of the Killbox 3 AI flow.
- Hawkeye 6 and Lancer 7 work the eastern/coastal SA-10 North problem.
- Devil 1, Eagle 5, and Lancer 7 initially use safe patrol STPT 3-4 outside SA-10 North's WEZ. If Alpha/Bravo are active, they stand off west until Plasma 3 completes its tasking. They advance to desired patrol STPT 5-6 only after SA-10 North is down and Alpha/Bravo are no longer factor; otherwise they remain at STPT 3-4.
- After the forward-patrol gate opens, Devil 1 and Eagle 5 may press Pohang on their briefed axes while Lancer 7 maintains the north-northeast block.
- Spade 7 remains over the killboxes and does not chase threats away from its primary station area.
- Mudhen 7 and Cyborg 5 sequence onto the same Killbox 1 sector; Cyborg 5 checks in for Mudhen 7's target-area lowdown.
- All AI flights continue their attacks regardless of Alpha/Bravo status and use low-altitude pop-up/standoff profiles if either site becomes factor.
